# Code of conduct

Jisr is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful of different experiences and viewpoints
- Giving constructive, specific feedback
- Accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for users and the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, publishing private information, or deliberately disrupting project
spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and
other contributions that do not follow these standards. Serious or repeated
violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

To report conduct concerns privately, use the contact information at
[abdulrahman.alaasi.dev](https://abdulrahman.alaasi.dev). Reports will be
reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all Jisr project spaces and whenever someone is
officially representing the project.
